# Dataset attribution

BlueLink's article-title database is derived from English Wikipedia data published by the Wikimedia Foundation. Wikipedia text and data are available under their applicable Wikimedia licensing terms; see <https://dumps.wikimedia.org/legal.html> and <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights>.

BlueLink displays live Wikipedia pages without removing Wikipedia's normal attribution or interface. The application does not claim ownership of article content.
